# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People speaking, editing, saving, and listening to their own notes in a desktop or phone browser. The primary workflow is a useful personal tool rather than a marketing page.

## Product Purpose

VCE2TXT turns speech into editable notes, keeps an explicitly pinned board in this browser, and offers optional password-protected AI help. The article reader adds listening to public articles without requiring an account or paid speech API.

## Brand Personality

Plainspoken, calm, practical. This isolated preview applies the user-approved Superdesign layout: a light three-column workspace with an indigo accent and Plus Jakarta Sans. Copy names the action and explains its outcome without promotional claims.

## Anti-references

Keep the three-column Superdesign workspace focused on real app features, without invented confidence scores, paid plans, users, or analytics. Do not restore its scroll hero or turn the working app into a landing page. Do not require AI sign-in for free browser features.

## Design Principles

- Keep the user's task immediately available; no introductory hero.
- Save deliberately: pinning is the boundary between a draft and a stored note.
- Explain which features use a remote service and which stay in this browser.
- Reuse working controls and the Phosphor icon vocabulary while matching the approved Superdesign layout.
- Give blocked or unsupported operations a useful next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The existing app provides labeled buttons and fields, keyboard shortcuts that avoid text fields, live status announcements, visible focus, and reduced-motion treatments. New controls should preserve those patterns and work on narrow phone screens.
